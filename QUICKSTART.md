# Your kid’s first session

Use this checklist with the original wedge-shaped Nintendo 2DS. You’ll need the console, a compatible charger, a stylus, an SD card for photos, and one compatible game your family owns.

## 1. Check the handheld

- [ ] Charge it and check that it stays on when unplugged. Test the shoulder buttons, sleep switch, touchscreen, and cartridge slot while you can still return it.
- [ ] Test all directions on the Circle Pad and let go to see if it returns to center. If it drifts, try **System Settings → Other Settings → Circle Pad** and retest before buying a part.
- [ ] Keep any existing saves or personal media you want to preserve. If the used console asks for a PIN you don’t know, ask the seller or contact Nintendo Support before changing settings.

## 2. Pick a game and a few things to make

- [ ] Turn the system off, insert a compatible cartridge, and turn it back on. Launch the game and check that you can save and resume.
- [ ] Open **Nintendo 3DS Camera** and take a photo or short video. View it again in the app.
- [ ] Open **Nintendo 3DS Sound**, record a silly sound, and play it back. Open **Mii Maker** and save a character.
- [ ] Drag the creative app icons where your kid can find them on the HOME Menu. Review **System Settings → Parental Controls**, including browser access, and decide together how photos and recordings can be shared.

## 3. Do the handoff offline

- [ ] From the HOME Menu, open **HOME Menu Settings** at the upper left of the lower screen. Find **Wireless Communication**, select **Off**, and check that the wireless light is off. [Nintendo’s wireless settings instructions](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/253/p/605/c/991)
- [ ] Power the 2DS off and back on. With wireless still off, launch the game, resume the save, and reopen the photo, recording, and Mii. If a pick requires a connection, leave it out of the offline lineup.
- [ ] Give your kid one thing to try. “Can you give your Mii a voice?” is enough to get started.

That’s the stock setup. If you later want to add drawing or animation homebrew, follow the live guide for your model and system version. [3DS Hacks Guide](https://3ds.hacks.guide/)
